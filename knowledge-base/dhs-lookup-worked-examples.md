## Method recap (what these examples prove)
1. GC on the DHS page must match the customer's data badge — DuoTec HE vs HE A differ.
2. Build-date splits (Vaillant control box) and output splits change the part.
3. Sub-parts (motor/cartridge/body; sensor/adaptor) sit adjacent — diagram number + MPN both go on the order.
4. Where manual and DHS numbers conflict (Intergas ignition), stop and phone.
