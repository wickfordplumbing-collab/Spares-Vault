# DHS & Suppliers — verified routes to the right part

## DHS — Direct Heating Spares (https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/)
Verified 19 Oct 2026 via homepage, Spares Finder and FAQ (https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/frequently-asked-questions).

- **What it is:** UK spares specialist (Chorley, Lancashire, trading since 2006 per FAQ), says 20,000+ spares in stock, next-day delivery available, 7pm order cut-off claimed on homepage.
- **Lookup works three ways (per FAQ):**
  1. **Part number** in the search box — "easiest way if you have a part number".
  2. **Spares Finder / appliance selector** (https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/?page=spares-finder): Brand → Appliance → Category → Sub-category → Part. Brand list verified includes Baxi, Potterton, Main, Vaillant, Glowworm, Ideal Domestic, Keston, Morco, Heatline, Viessmann, Intergas, Alpha, Ariston, Ferroli, Vokera, Ravenheat, Biasi and many more.
  3. Phone **0330 123 3707** / live chat (7 days a week per FAQ) if model/GC not found.
- **GC-number pages:** appliance pages state the GC number and list parts that fit that appliance — verified example: Ideal Classic FF 350 page gives "GC Number: 41-391-96" and lists parts with manufacturer part numbers (e.g. Ideal 171461 Fan Assembly Kit) — https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/category/gas-boiler-uk/brand/ideal-domestic/appliance/ideal-classic-ff-350-ide226 . *Prices/stock on that page are live data — never copy them into notes; check at order time.*
- **Categories** used by DHS (useful as a checklist of part families): Air Pressure Switch, Auto Air Vent, Burner, Condensate Trap, Diverter Valve, Electrodes, Expansion Vessels, Fans, Flow Switch, Flue, Gas Valves, Heat Exchanger, Ignition Unit, Orings/Washers/Gaskets, Printed Circuit Board, Pumps, Pressure Relief & Safety Valves, Thermistor… full list: https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/category/gas-boiler-uk
- **Manuals:** DHS also hosts free boiler manual downloads by brand (e.g. Heatline page https://www.dhsspares.co.uk/boiler-manuals/heatline) — cross-check against the manufacturer library where one exists.
- **Accounts/pricing:** FAQ states you do **not** need an account to buy; DHS sells to trade and general public; trade credit account available by application; **for trade prices, phone** — website prices shown inc VAT on retail pages. So: Wickford's DHS terms/pricing depend on our own trade account/login — `confirm our account terms before quoting margins`. Trade counter (Chorley) prices may differ from website (per FAQ).
- **Genuine vs alternative:** DHS sells "brand new genuine", "alternative/similar" and "refurbished/tested" options, labelled on product pages (FAQ). For gas-carrying / combustion parts, prefer genuine manufacturer parts and say so (see Baxi genuine-parts liability note in README).

## PartsArena (Wolseley)
- Manufacturer-backed parts-identification database (Baxi directs users to it: https://www.baxi.co.uk/professional/services/spare-parts).
- How it works (Wolseley guide / PartsArena): search manufacturer, model, GC number, part number or keyword → interactive exploded diagram → tap position hotspot → part description, photo, manufacturer/stockist codes → basket/share. Source: https://www.wolseley.co.uk/wcsstore/Wolseley/Attachment/how-to-guides/how-to-guide-parts-arena-mobile.pdf ; https://www.partsarena.com/partsarena-pro-for-field-engineers/
- Access: aimed at Wolseley trade account holders / heating professionals; PartsArena Pro app adds offline use, manuals & barcode scanning. If Wickford has a Wolseley account, this is the fastest diagram route for Baxi-group and many other brands.
