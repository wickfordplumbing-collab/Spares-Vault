# Parts Glossary — what it does, how it fails, confusions

Generic method only. Test values, resistances and voltages are **in the manual for that appliance** — anything not cited is marked `unverified — check manual`. Testing inside a boiler is Gas Safe / competent-person work.

| Part | What it does | Typical failure symptoms | Identification / test (engineer) | Common confusions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PCB (main control board)** | Controls ignition sequence, fan, pump, gas valve, sensors; stores fault codes | Dead boiler, random lockouts, codes for several different parts, burnt tracks/water damage | Visual (tracks, capacitors, water), correct supply in/out per manual wiring diagram. Diagnose *last* after proving the parts it supplies | Replacing PCB to cure a sensor/fan fault; note some PCBs need configuration/setup (Ideal F09/L09 = unconfigured PCB per Ideal Logic guide) |
| **Fan** | Pushes flue gases out, draws air in; speed feedback (Hall) on modern boilers | No ignition, fan fault codes (e.g. Vaillant F.32, Worcester C6, Ideal F03 — cited in brands file), noisy bearings | Runs at pre-purge? Correct voltage from PCB, free rotation, Hall/speed signal per manual | Fan vs PCB (no supply = PCB/harness); seized fan vs blocked flue |
| **Gas valve** | Meters gas to burner under PCB control (solenoid / stepper) | Spark but no flame, flame failure, some F.26/F.61-type codes on Vaillant (gas valve actuation) | Gas Safe only: inlet/working pressures, solenoid continuity, correct gas restrictor for model/fuel | **Never second-guess a gas valve from a code alone**; wrong-gas-valve / wrong restrictor is itself a listed cause of Vaillant F.28 |
| **Diverter valve (body)** | Combi: directs primary water to heating or plate heat exchanger for DHW | Heating but no DHW (or lukewarm), DHW but rads warm during hot water, sticking mid-position | Engineer checks spindle movement / cartridge, motor operation separately | Body vs **motor/actuator** vs cartridge kit — three different orders; scaled/stuck cartridge mimics motor failure |
| **Diverter motor / actuator** | Drives diverter valve spindle | One service (CH or DHW) missing, motor silent or clicking | Voltage at motor during DHW demand, spindle moves by hand (isolated/drained as manual says) | Assuming whole valve needed when only motor/cartridge has failed — and vice versa |
| **Plate heat exchanger (DHW)** | Transfers primary heat to tap water in a combi | DHW lukewarm/scaly fluctuation, heating OK, poor flow, temperature swings as it scales/blocks | Temperature in/out, flow; primary side blockage vs domestic scale (hard-water areas) | Descaling vs replacing; also check thermistor and flow sensor before condemning it |
| **Main (primary) heat exchanger** | Transfers burner heat to primary water | Kettling/noise, overheat codes, leaks, poor circulation, flue temp high | Engineer: flow/return temps, circulation, combustion check | Kettling is often sludge/limescale or pump/circulation, not a failed exchanger — treat water first |
| **Pump (circulator)** | Moves primary water round heating / boiler | No flow, overheat, noisy, F.75-type (Vaillant, with sensor), heating cold spots | Spins freely, voltage present, speed setting, bleed/air; check AAV | Pump failed vs airlock vs blockage vs PCB pump supply |
| **Expansion vessel** | Absorbs heating water expansion | Pressure rises sharply when hot, PRV discharges, pressure low cold / high hot, water from vessel valve | Isolated & drained: air charge vs manual spec (`check manual` for bar value), water at Schrader = diaphragm split | Re-pressurising system repeatedly masks a flat/split vessel; PRV then fouls |
| **PRV (pressure relief / safety valve)** | Vents system at set pressure (commonly 3 bar — `verify per manual`) | Dripping discharge pipe, pressure loss | Only replace after finding *why* it lifted (vessel, overfilling, overheat) | PRV is often victim, not culprit; a vented PRV may not reseat due to debris |
| **Pressure sensor / switch** | Tells PCB water pressure (electronic sensor) or proves pressure/flow (switch) | Low-pressure codes with gauge OK (or vice versa), F.75 Vaillant, E119 Baxi family | Compare gauge vs displayed pressure, sensor output/resistance per manual | Sensor vs actually-low pressure vs blocked sensing point |
| **Thermistor / NTC sensor** | Temperature sensor (flow, return, DHW, flue); resistance falls as temperature rises | Implausible temps, thermistor codes (Ideal F04/F05), cycling, DHW temp swings | Isolated, disconnected: resistance at known temp vs **that manual's resistance table** — do not use a generic curve. General principle source: https://flintfix.com/ntc-thermistor-resistance (non-manufacturer — method only) | Flow vs return vs DHW NTC are different parts/positions; clip-on sensors reading loose mimic failure |
| **Flow switch / DHW flow sensor (turbine)** | Detects tap opened, fires boiler for DHW | No DHW (heating fine), no DHW demand icon, intermittent on low flow | Demand registers when tap opens? Clean filter/turbine, connector | vs diverter fault: if boiler doesn't even *register* DHW demand, suspect sensor first |
| **Ignition electrode** | Spark to light gas | Clicking/no ignition, spark but weak/misdirected, ignition lockout (Ideal L02-type) | Gap/condition/lead per manual, spark seen/heard via engineer's method | Electrode vs ignition transformer/PCB generator vs gas supply |
| **Flame rectification / ionisation probe** | Proves flame via ionisation current | Lights then cuts out after seconds, F.28/F.29-type behaviour | Probe clean/positioned in flame, lead continuity, earth integrity (poor earth is a listed F.28 cause on Vaillant) | Often same assembly as ignition electrode on some models — check diagram, don't assume separate |
| **Burner** | Mixes/burns gas at injector, flame across exchanger | Poor flame picture, ignition pop, CO concerns — **engineer/combustion analysis only** | Visual + analyser by Gas Safe engineer | Never sell burner parts on a phone description alone; confirm model + fuel (NG/LPG injectors differ) |
| **Injector** | Sizes gas flow into burner | Wrong flame, over/under-gassing after fuel conversion | Size stamped; must match manual for fuel/model | NG vs LPG injector mix-ups — safety-critical identification |
| **Condensate trap / sump** | Collects acidic condensate, water seal to flue | Gurgling, ignition failure in cold/blocked cases, leaks, F.28 listed cause (blocked condensate duct, Vaillant) | Trap clear, pipe falls/frozen section outside, sump seals | Frozen external condensate is a *user-guide* check in cold weather; internal blockage is engineer work |
| **Air pressure switch (APS)** — older designs | Proves fan/flue pull before ignition | Fan runs, no ignition sequence advance, intermittent in wind | Tubes split/blocked, diaphragm, contacts per manual | Modern boilers may use fan speed feedback instead — don't order an APS that the model doesn't have |
| **Flue parts (terminals, bends, seals)** | Safe removal of flue gases / air supply | EA-type (Worcester: blocked flue listed), weather damage, leaking joints | Only manufacturer flue system for that boiler; seals condition | Mixing flue brands/systems is not permitted — identify by boiler model |
| **Seals / gaskets / O-rings / washers** | Seal combustion, water and gas joints disturbed in service | Leaks after a repair, combustion door seal degraded | Replace seals disturbed during service as manual instructs; check if included in the main part's kit | Ordering a heat exchanger/valve without the seal kit the diagram lists separately — a classic van-stock gap |
| **DHW diaphragm** (older combis) | Water pressure moves diverter pin in older designs | DHW fails gradually, pin not moving | Perished/split diaphragm in water section | Modern boilers use flow turbines instead — check design generation |
| **Display / user control / clock** | User interface, times/temps | Blank display, buttons dead, heating schedule faults | Power to boiler present? Fuse, harness; external controls (room stat, receiver) proved first | Blank display ≠ PCB every time — check fuse/power/external controls first |
| **Fuse / transformer (on PCB or separate)** | Protects / steps down supply for controls | Dead boiler, no display | Engineer checks board fuse & supply per wiring diagram | Repeated fuse blowing = find the short (pump/fan/valve) before feeding it fuses |
| **Auto air vent (AAV)** | Vents air from boiler/system high point | Gurgling, air, pressure swings, weeping cap | Cap condition, leak traces | — |
| **Pressure gauge** | Shows system pressure | Needle stuck/false reading vs digital display | Compare with sensor reading/external gauge | Gauge fault mimics low-pressure faults |
| **Isolation / filling loop parts** | Fill and isolate boiler | Can't re-pressurise, passing valves | External to boiler on many installs — may not be a boiler spare at all | Don't sell a boiler part for a system-side filling-loop problem |
